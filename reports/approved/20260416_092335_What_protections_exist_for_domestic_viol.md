# What protections exist for domestic violence victims in EU law?

The protections for domestic violence victims in EU law include several key directives and regulations:

1. **EU Victims’ Directive**: This directive establishes minimum standards on the rights, protection, and support of victims of crime, including victims of domestic violence. It replaced the Framework Decision on the standing of victims in criminal proceedings. The excerpt states: "In May 2011, the European Commission adopted a package of legislative proposals to enhance the rights of victims of crime, which includes the EU Victims’ Directive establishing minimum standards on the rights, protection and support of victims of crime."

2. **Proposed Directive on Combating Violence Against Women and Domestic Violence**: This is a comprehensive legal instrument aimed at tackling violence against women and domestic violence across the EU. It ensures that all victims benefit from the same comprehensive set of measures of protection, support, and access to justice. The excerpt states: "Most recently, a political agreement has been reached on the proposal for a directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on combating violence against women and domestic violence."

3. **European Protection Order**: This regulation is designed to provide mutual recognition of protection measures in civil matters, which is particularly beneficial for victims of domestic violence as they move between Member States. The excerpt mentions: "Specifically, the package also included a Regulation on mutual recognition of protection measures in civil matters, which is in place and should be of benefit to victims of domestic violence."

4. **Istanbul Convention**: The EU has signed the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence, which further strengthens the legal framework for protecting victims. The excerpt states: "The EU has also signed the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence (the Istanbul Convention)."

These legal instruments collectively aim to provide a robust framework for the protection and support of victims of domestic violence in the EU.
