# What does the Istanbul Convention require of police responding to violence against women?

The Istanbul Convention requires police to respond to all forms of violence promptly and appropriately by offering adequate and immediate protection to victims. The excerpt states: "Article 50 of the Istanbul Convention, which obliges parties to the convention to take the necessary measures to ensure that the police respond to all forms of violence 'promptly and appropriately by offering adequate and immediate protection to victims'."
