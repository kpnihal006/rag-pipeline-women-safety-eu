# What is the Istanbul Convention?

The Istanbul Convention is an international agreement that legally defines violence against women and establishes a comprehensive framework of legal and policy measures for preventing such violence, supporting victims, and punishing perpetrators. The excerpt states: "The Istanbul Convention on preventing and combatting violence against women and domestic violence is an international agreement that legally defines violence against women and establishes a comprehensive framework of legal and policy measures for preventing such violence, supporting victims and punishing perpetrators."
